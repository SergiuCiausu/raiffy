# Raiffy · Design guidelines

A summary of the UI language used in `raiffy.html`, the concept landing page for **Raiffy**, an AI financial assistant for Raiffeisen Bank Romania. Any new page, section or component should follow it.

> The page feels premium because of **many small, correct details**, not big type. Headlines stay modest. The richness comes from cards with miniature product UI inside, light used as a material (soft glows), hairline borders and quiet secondary text.

## 1. Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Every feature gets a built UI fragment in HTML/CSS (chat with a comparison table, repayment slider, savings list, bank statement, document checklist). Never an icon with a paragraph.
2. **Light is a material.** Color shows up as diffused yellow/amber glow behind content. No flat gradient backgrounds, no gradient text.
3. **Quiet type, loud craft.** Small, tight, confident type. The craft lives in the visuals.
4. **Honest finance.** Options are shown side by side with the same amount, term and fields. **Raiffy recommends one financing option and always says why**: the reasons, plus when another option is the better pick. Every option stays visible and choosable, every number has a source pill, and the customer makes the final choice. All figures are illustrative and the copy is plausible and specific: real lei amounts, dates and Romanian places. No lorem ipsum, no emoji, no stock images.

## 2. Modes

The page is **Mixed** and switches mode exactly once: a **Nocturne** hero hands off to **Daylight** for everything else. Set the mode with `data-mode="nocturne" | "daylight"` on a section or the body.

| Token | Daylight | Nocturne |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FAF9F7` | `#08080B` (hero base `#07070A`) |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#0E0E13` |
| `--surface-2` | `#F4F3F0` | `#15151C` |
| `--border` | `rgba(20,20,20,.07)` | `rgba(255,255,255,.06)` |
| `--border-strong` | `rgba(20,20,20,.12)` | `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` |
| `--text-1` | `#1A1A1A` | `#F4F4F6` |
| `--text-2` | `#5C5C5F` | `rgba(244,244,246,.66)` |
| `--text-3` | `#8E8E93` | `rgba(244,244,246,.42)` |

Never use pure black or pure white for text. Use three levels: primary, secondary, tertiary. The alternate section background (the "For the bank" section) is `#F4F3F0`.

## 3. Color and accent

- **One accent pair, close to the bank's yellow:** `--accent-a: #FFD23F` (yellow) and `--accent-b: #FF9A3D` (amber). Don't introduce other hues.
- On Daylight, the accent is **light, never text**: blooms, focus halos (`0 0 0 4px rgba(255,210,63,.35)`), highlighted rows (`#FFF8DC`, `#FFF3C4`) and the logo mark.
- **The primary "ink" is `#1A1A1A`.** Daylight uses it for filled buttons, active segments, checkboxes and chart bars.
- **Slider fills are yellow light** (`--accent-a`), with a 1px `--accent-b` edge under the fill so it reads on the light track (agreed 29 Oct 2026). The thumb stays white with its border, and keyboard focus is the ink ring. Selection elsewhere stays ink. Selection is shown with dark ink, not color.
- Badges/pills: 11px on a 12% tint of their color (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--c) 12%, transparent)`). Deep amber text (`#8A6A00`, `#7A5A00`) sits on yellow tints.
- Semantic green appears only for checks (`#2E7D4F`).
- `--danger: #B42318` is for form errors and failing checks only (`login.html`, `console.html`), never for decoration or "bad" options.
- **Never:** gradient text, rainbow gradients, purple/blue, neon outlines.

## 4. Typography

Font: **Geist** 400/500/600, with **Geist Mono** for amounts, times, codes, keyboard hints and the thinking panel. Loaded from Google Fonts.

| Token | Size | LH | Weight | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| h1 | **36px** (30px under 640px) | 1.1 | 600 | -0.035em |
| h2 | 28px (24px) | 1.15 | 600 | -0.03em |
| h3 | 20px (18px) | 1.3 | 600 | -0.02em |
| h4 | 16px | 1.4 | 600 | -0.01em |
| lead | 17px | 1.55 | 400 | n/a, `max-width: 46ch`, `--text-2` |
| body | 15px | 1.6 | 400 | n/a |
| sm | 14px | 1.55 | 400 | card descriptions, `--text-2` |
| xs | 13px | 1.45 | 500 | buttons, nav, labels |
| micro | 11–12px | ~1.35 | 500 | inside mockups, pills |

- **No heading above 36px.** Build hierarchy with weight, color and spacing.
- Eyebrows: 11.5px, weight 500, uppercase, tracking `.08em`, `--text-3`. In the hero, add a glowing 6px yellow dot in front.
- Headings use `text-wrap: balance` and paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`. Keep lines at 60ch or less.
- Numbers use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Money is written `8,000 lei` and `696 lei / month`.

## 5. Layout and spacing

- Container: 1200px, gutter 24px (16px on mobile). Spacing snaps to a 4px grid.
- Section padding: 96px vertically (64px on mobile). Header block to content: 48px (32px on mobile). h1 to lead: 16px. Lead to CTAs: 28px.
- Section headers are **left-aligned** (`max-width: 620px`). Only the final CTA is centered.
- **Bento:** a 12-column grid with a 16px gap. The features use a zigzag of 7/5, then 5/7, then a full-width 12 card. The bank section uses 3 equal cards. Under 860px, everything collapses to one column.
- Deep-dive: a `5fr / 7fr` split (rules list on the left, live simulator card on the right), gap 48px.
- Breakpoints: 480, 640, 760, 860, 960, 1100px, written mobile-first as `min-width` queries (see §12).

## 6. Surfaces

**Daylight card:** `#FFF` background, 1px `--border`, radius **20px**, shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.04)`. The anatomy is a visual zone on top (260–320px tall, `aria-hidden`), then a body with 24px padding, an h4, an 8px gap and an sm description.

**Fragments (`.frag`)** inside visuals: white, hairline border, radius 14px, shadow `0 8px 30px rgba(20,20,40,.08)` (the heaviest allowed, except glows).

**Nocturne hero:** layered backdrop from bottom to top: base, haze (radial amber glows), smoke (SVG turbulence, screen blend), grid (192/32px lines), ghost UI (faint task card, checklist, stat, sparkline, icon dock), isometric art, grain (7% overlay), then the copy. Every layer is masked around one focus point (`--focus-x: 72%`, `--focus-y: 50%`) placed on the art, never behind the headline. A 140px bottom fade hands off into the page.

**Isometric stack (hero art):** three slabs (`rotateX(58deg) rotateZ(-45deg)`) tell the story: a comparison table, then a repayment simulation, then a glowing "You decide" chip. Slabs are 62px apart, with thin extruded sides, a floor glow and two floating notes. The stack orbits toward the cursor (iso-tilt) and gently bobs.

## 7. Product vignettes

The rules every vignette follows:
- Micro-type at 11–13px. Dense but legible, with product-specific copy.
- **Bleed:** at least one fragment runs past the card edge and fades out with a mask (`.fade-r`, `.fade-b`, `.fade-br`).
- **Depth:** dimmed duplicates (`.dim`, opacity .35) sit behind the focused element.
- **One moment of interaction** per vignette: a cursor arrow, a selected row, a slider knob with a yellow halo, or an active segment.
- **One bloom** per visual: a yellow+amber radial (`.bloom`, `--x/--y/--size`), placed off-center and partly clipped by the card. It's drawn with stepped gradient stops, not `filter: blur`.
- Raiffy's avatar: a dark ink circle with a yellow compass icon (`.ava-raiffy`).
- Icons: Lucide (pinned `lucide@0.468.0`), 11–16px.

## 8. Components

- **Nav:** fixed, 60px. Logo (24px yellow rounded mark plus "Raiffy *by Raiffeisen Bank*"), 4 links at 13.5px, then a ghost button and a dark button. No language switch: everything is English for now. It starts transparent over the hero, turns `.is-dark` (blurred) while over the hero and `.is-light` (blurred `rgba(250,249,247,.82)` with a hairline border) after it. Links and the ghost button hide under 860px.
- **Buttons:** 40px tall (34px in the nav), radius 10px, 13px at weight 500. On touch screens every button, chip and icon button reaches a 44px hit area (§12).
  - `.btn-dark`: ink background with white text. `.btn-ghost`: transparent with a `--border-strong` hairline.
  - Hero: `.btn-glow` is a 44px pill, 12px uppercase 600 text, a cream-to-yellow fill and a warm outer glow (the hover glow is on `::after` via opacity). `.btn-line` is a 44px pill with a translucent hairline.
  - Chips/quick replies: `.qbtn`, a 12px pill on the landing page. In product UI (`chat.html` onward), chips are 10px-radius rectangles (see Product UI rules).
- **Rules list:** a 28px icon tile, a 14.5px h4 and a 13.5px secondary text, separated by hairline rows.
- **KPI strip:** 3 cells in one bordered card with hairline dividers. Values at 20px/600 with tabular numbers.
- **Inputs:** a custom range with a 4px track (ink fill through `--p`) and an 18px white thumb that gets a yellow halo while active.
- **Swipe deck** (`swipe.html`): a 3/4 card stack, at most 3 cards in the DOM, with each card below 0.95 scale and 22px lower. Cards follow the Daylight card anatomy with a vignette zone on top. Drag tilts up to ±30° and commits at 30% of the card width or on a flick, and a short drag snaps back with overshoot. The stamps stay in the accent language: **Save** is ink on `#FFF3C4` with the yellow halo, and **Skip** is a `--text-2` hairline. No red/green. Controls are equal-size "Skip" (ghost) and "Save" (dark) buttons plus undo, arrow keys and an `aria-live` announcement. A large content set gets one vignette per topic (not per card) and `.qbtn` topic chips, with the active chip in ink. Under reduced motion, there's no tilt and a fade replaces the fly-out.
- **Recommendation:** exactly one option per comparison carries a `.rec-badge`: "Recommended" with a sparkles icon, ink on `#FFF3C4` with the yellow halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(255,210,63,.35)`. The recommended option gets a light tint (`#FFFCF0` column, `#FFFDF5` card, with `#FFF3C4` where it meets a highlighted row). The other options keep the same size and fields. Under it, a "Why I recommend…" block lists 2-3 reasons with check icons, then one line in `--text-3` saying when the other option is the better pick. The rule is deterministic and lives in code (cheapest option that fits, with a near-tie going to the fixed rate), never in the LLM. In live simulators, the badge and reasons move as the inputs change, with no transition.
- **Follow-up suggestions:** every answer ships with 2-3 **pre-generated follow-up replies**, shown as `.chip` buttons above the composer, so the customer can keep going without typing.
  - **Tab** with an empty composer fills it with the first suggestion. Tab again moves to the next one, and after the last one Tab moves focus as usual. **Enter** sends.
  - The next suggestion carries a mono `Tab` kbd hint, hidden on coarse pointers.
  - The placeholder reads "Ask a follow-up, or press Tab for a suggestion" (just "Ask a follow-up" on coarse pointers), and each fill is announced through the live region.
  - For a `detail` answer, one suggestion goes deeper into the same product and one opens a related Raiffeisen solution.
  - Only the latest finished answer has suggestions, and they never sit in the answer. The answer's footer (`.gfoot`) holds evidence and answer actions only: numbered source tags, "Ask a specialist", copy and vote. Choice chips ("Which of these is closest?") and "Skip, show typical examples" are suggestions too (`web/lib/suggestions.ts`).
  - Hovering or focusing a source tag lights it and its row in "Sources in this chat" (`--tint-hl`), and an open Context panel scrolls to that row. A tag with no page to open (sample data) is a button that also opens the panel. **Up arrow** in an empty composer brings back the last question to edit.
- **Attaching a statement** (`chat.html` `#attach`): the paperclip attaches the chosen sample customer's last complete month as a `.file-chip` ("Statement, September 2026", mono "Sample"), removable with its cross. No sample customer chosen opens Context to choose one. The sent question carries the same chip. Never a real upload in this preview.
- **Build my plan** (`chat.html` mkplan): a `.plan-check` row at the foot of a simulation builds the plan from what the simulator shows; the `.plan-card` follows the answer (goal, monthly, total and DAE as estimates, the balance bars, the catalog source, the indicative label). When the catalog can't back a plan, the row says why in one sentence and the simulation stays an estimate.
- **Chat names and pins** (`chat.html` `#rename` and `PIN_META`, `convex/chatThreads.ts`): a pencil beside the title turns it into a `.title-input` (Enter or leaving saves, Escape cancels). An answer with a card gets a pin icon button in its actions ("Pin to context", pressed when pinned). "Pinned" in Context lists pins from every chat, newest first, as `.pin` rows: the card's icon on a `--tint-rec` tile, its title, and one mono line naming what it covers (no figure the customer didn't give). Pressing a pin opens its answer, or its chat; the cross unpins.
- **Starter vignettes** (`web/components/agent/StarterVignettes.tsx`): each starter card in the live chat's empty state gets its own built picture in the 78px `.mini` zone, drawn from the shape of its answer (term slots, a checklist, debit and credit cards, a two-bank table, a credit-line meter, packages, routes, a document stack). One fragment bleeds right, with one interaction moment (an ink slot or card, a `.sel` row, a haloed knob). The live chat shows no figures there: values are neutral `--track` bars, because every number in `/chat` needs a cited source. Dimmed extras drop under 600px. When motion is allowed, each picture plays once (30fps timing, about 1.4s, 80ms apart) each time the empty state shows, starting only when its card is on screen: parts rise or slide in, checks pop, bars fill, and the selection steps onto its target. Moving parts carry `data-beat` (`starter-motion/timing.ts`) and play as Web Animations using transform and opacity only: fills scale from the left, the knob rides a rail as wide as the fill, and selections are layers that fade (`.mini-hl` for rows, `.mini-on` for ink slots), so playback never lays out or repaints a row. `will-change` is set only while a clip plays. The resting CSS is the last frame. Under reduced motion no animation is created. Remotion is dev-only: `pnpm remotion:studio` previews the same animations and `pnpm remotion:render` exports them.
- **Inputs request** (`chat.html` `.inputs-form`): when an answer needs details the customer hasn't given, Raiffy asks in one turn: a short line, then a `.gcard` titled "A few details" with at most 3 fields. Amounts are a 40px `--radius-btn` box with mono tabular input and the unit (`lei`, `months`, `%`) in `--text-2`. Focus uses the ink border and `--halo`. Choices are 32px rectangles that turn ink when pressed. Hints sit under the field at 13px. The footer says "Or answer in your own words below." and has one ink "Continue". Inputs are 16px under 640px so iOS doesn't zoom. A "Skip, show typical examples" chip sits with the follow-ups. Once sent, or once a newer answer arrives, the card is disabled on `--surface-3`. When two patterns are equally likely, Raiffy asks "Which of these is closest?" with one chip per pattern instead.
- **Answer list card** (`web` `AnswerText`, `.list-card`): a list of two or more items in an answer renders inside a `.gcard`, not as loose bullets. Header: a list icon and a title (a short "Conditions to check:" lead-in line moves into it, otherwise "Overview" for pattern 12 and "Key points" elsewhere), with the count in mono on the right ("5 items", "4 steps"). Rows are separated by hairlines, 11px × 14px padding, no bullet markers: the item's bold lead becomes the label (weight 500, ink, colon dropped) and the rest a 13px `--text-2` description, with citation chips kept in place. Numbered lists get a 20px mono number tile in a `--border-strong` square. It stays a real `<ul>`/`<ol>` named by the header, fades in with `.gcard.enter` only when motion is allowed, and a one-item list stays inline. Items an answer card already shows (by name or bold label) are left out of the text list; a list left empty disappears with its title line.
- **Answer cards in the live chat** (`web/components/agent/AnswerCards.tsx`): the demo cards' markup, filled from the answer instead of scripted data. A comparison table takes 2 to 4 options (wider tables scroll inside `.tbl-wrap`); an empty cell reads "Not in the sources" in `--text-3`. Every figure ends in its source chip number (`.cite`). A fact is one short figure with its unit, in English (at most 48 characters), set in the sans with tabular figures like the `.cmp` cells; its context goes in the label. Routes follow chat.html: two sit side by side (`.opts`), three or four stack as full-width rows like `.saves`, the recommended one first, and each route's figures sit label-above-value like `.plan-figs`. A card with a recommendation ends in `.rec-why` ("Why I recommend…", reasons, when another fits better, "the choice is yours"); one without ends in a `.gc-note` saying the figures are dated public information, not an offer. The simulation card reuses the demo's `.sim` controls (amount range, term buttons) with a single option spanning the row: the product with its cited rate in mono, the monthly payment large, then total repaid and interest as `.kv` rows. Its note says whether it starts from the customer's figures or an example, and that fees and the APR aren't included. A rate range shows as "956 – 1,269 lei / month" with "At the lowest and the highest published rate" under it. With an income, a "Share of your income" row follows, and a published limit row only when a source states one. Neither is coloured: there is no pass or fail.
- **Where answer cards sit** (`web/lib/card-placement.ts`, `chat.html` `cardAfter`): a card goes inside the answer, after the paragraph that introduces it, never stacked first. That is a paragraph ending in a colon ("Here they are side by side:") that isn't a list's title, else the first paragraph citing one of the card's sources, else the lead-in. While the answer types out, the card fades in (300ms, 80ms apart for several) once its paragraph has finished, and the text continues below it. Reopened answers and reduced motion show everything in place. Booking, inputs and memory cards stay after the text. Cards keep the full width; only the text keeps the 64ch measure.
- **Counter-offer win rate** (below the queue in `console.html#reviews`, `web/components/staff/ReviewsConsole.tsx`): one section, never a card per breakdown. Before the first counter-offer it's a single empty-state card (`.wr-empty`) that says what will appear and how it's counted. With data, it shows one `.kpis` row (win rate, counter-offers made, accepted of decided, average gap), then `.wr-grid`: one breakdown table whose group (Resolution, Product, Competitor, Segment) is a `.qbtn` switch in the card header, and "Why customers said no" beside it. The counting rule is one `.wr-note` footnote. Rates and gaps are ink, never coloured as wins or losses.
- **Price and counter-offer card** (`chat.html` `offerHTML`, `web/components/agent/OfferCard.tsx`, from `docs/shared/pricing.md`): three parts. **Conditional price rows** (`.prices`/`.price-row`): the price without conditions first, then one row per condition, all in the same type and weight. The condition is the row's label ("With salary transfer"), and the cost of what it requires sits in the row's description and inside its total and DAE. A condition the customer already meets gets a "You already qualify" pill. **Total cost side by side** (`.vs`): Raiffeisen and the competitor at the same amount and term, with rate, upfront fee, DAE and total to repay, in mono, with no colour for winner or loser. **Counter-offer**: one lead total on `--tint-hl` with its validity, and "What changed" as a `.why-row.tip`. **Source pills** (`.src-pills`): "Sample pricing", the competitor's source and "Counter-offer · valid until …" or "Offer from Ana M. · valid until …". Accepting is labelled "(simulated)" until the official application flow exists. A specialist review is a dark button the customer taps ("Ask a specialist to review this"), never sent on their behalf. Band limits never appear on the card.
- **Spending card and "What Raiffy can see"** (`chat.html` `spendingHTML`, `web/components/agent/SpendingCard.tsx`, `AccessPanel.tsx`): the statement card's `.stmt`/`.line` rows, one per category, with the change against the period before as `.dsc` and a 2px `--text-3` share bar on a `--surface-2` track (no colour per category). One lead total on `--tint-hl`, insights as `.why-row.tip`, and a 13px note naming the data. While account data is synthetic, the head says "Sample data" and the note says it isn't the customer's account. The panel has Smart Mobile's six switches, in its order: accounts and overdraft, transactions, savings and deposits, pre-approved offers, loans, cards. They all start off and stay disabled until a sample account is chosen; changes apply from the next question. "Reset sample data" (a small ghost button) returns to the sample customer an admin assigned, with everything off. An account source is a chip without a link.
- **Thinking panel:** while Raiffy works, a dimmed panel above the answer (`#FBFAF8`, hairline border, radius 12px, Geist Mono 12px in `--text-3`) lists pre-written status lines, with the current one in `--text-2` and amber dots, and done lines ticked. When the text starts streaming, the panel folds into a `<details>` summary, "How I worked this out · 2 steps", that the customer can reopen. Then the answer streams word by word with a blinking caret.
- **Sign-in methods** (`login.html`): a dark "Continue with Raiffeisen" button with the yellow logo mark (an SSO placeholder, not wired), then equal ghost Google and Apple buttons with monochrome marks, an "or use your email" divider and an email magic-link form. Sign-up adds the required terms checkbox and a separate, unticked marketing-consent checkbox.
- **Raiffy inside Smart Mobile** (`smart-mobile.html`): a mobile-first app on the same tokens, shown full screen on a phone and in a 390×844 frame on desktop.
  - **Entry points:** an "Ask Raiffy" bar above the tab bar on Home, "Ask Raiffy about this" links (a `#FFFDF7` row with the Raiffy avatar and the pre-generated question in quotes), and the **Raiffy cards** on Home: one `#FFFDF7` card at a time with one bloom, a small title, a figure in bold and its source in `--text-3`, moved through with a swipe or the arrow buttons next to a mono "2 of 6" counter, and a × that offers "Not interested in this" or "Don't show these". A card the customer comes back to shows a bookmark line ("You looked at this on 28 Oct") with "Not interested" (ghost) and "Continue" (ink). No floating bubble.
  - **Routes card:** a recommendation's options as stacked rows (name, detail, figure on the right), the recommended one first with its badge and tint, then the "Why I recommend…" block and an optional handoff button.
  - **Raiffy sheet:** full height with a grab handle, a context chip on `#FFF8DC` ("From: Overdraft · 8,000 lei used"), tap chips above the composer.
  - **Compare on mobile:** one option per column, swiped with scroll snap, the recommended one first, with dots and "Swipe to compare".
  - **Handoff:** an ink "Apply in Smart Mobile" button with an arrow-up-right icon, a ghost secondary, and a lock note saying the customer signs in the app.
  - **One-time allow:** a card showing the object, with equal "Allow once" (ink) and "Not now" (ghost) buttons.
  - **Official screens** (apply, repay): white, no blooms, no Raiffy avatar, a "Smart Mobile" lock tag and a 4-step progress bar, so the customer can see they've left the assistant. Touch targets are at least 44px.
- **Human verification** (`chat.html`): an "Ask a specialist" `.vbtn` in each answer's footer and "Check my plan" on the simulator open an inline request panel (`.vreq`). It says what the specialist will see, has a "See what's sent" disclosure, an optional note, and the honest wait. The status comes back as a `.vstat` line: waiting (grey), confirmed or edited with the reviewer's name and time (check green), and "Changed after the specialist's check" (yellow tint) when the inputs move. Edited text shows added words on `#FFF3C4` and removed words struck through. In the live app (`web/components/agent/AnswerCheck.tsx`) the panel promises no response time, and the line reads waiting (with a reference the customer can quote), confirmed (`.ok`), corrected (`.ok`, with the specialist's note in quotes) or sent back (`.changed`, with the reason). The specialist's side (`console.html#reviews/<id>`, `web/components/staff/AnswerCheckCase.tsx`) is the three-column `.ws`: the masked conversation with the answer under review on `.bub-r.flagged`, the answer as the customer saw it with its sources and the decision as radios, and "Built from". Answer checks and counter-offers share one queue, with type chips on the left and Open/Closed on the right.
- **Console** (`console.html`): Daylight only, with a 240px sidebar (nav with ink count badges for work that needs you) and a demo-only Operator/Admin switch. The exit to the chat is a full-width ghost button (`.back-chat`, arrow-left) at the top of the sidebar footer (`.me`), never a nav row, so it never reads as a page in the area. The same applies to `admin.html`. In the live admin pages, a section whose data isn't connected yet is one card that lists what will appear (`.micro` lines with a dashed-circle icon), never a row of zeros; read-only pages say so in their intro instead of showing disabled edit controls.
  - **Tables:** hairline rows, 11px uppercase headers, mono tabular numbers, whole rows clickable with a real link in the first cell. New rows get a 3px yellow inset on the left.
  - **Dated-value row** (`.dv`): name and what it means, the value in mono, then the source pill, start date, owner and review date always together. Past review date gets a `--danger` pill and a `#FFFCF0` row. Pending changes show under the value.
  - **Diffs:** added text or values on `#FFF3C4`, removed struck through in `--text-3`, never red/green.
  - **Checks** (`.chk`): a dot (green check, yellow minus for a warning, `--danger` cross for a failure) plus a title and one line of why. A failing check disables the action it guards.
  - **Editor sheet:** a native `<dialog>` from the right, 520px. Value, start date, required source, a note, and a live impact preview table where changed rows are tinted `#FFF8DC`.
  - **Review workspace:** conversation, answer and "built from" columns at 1440px or more, two columns below that, one column under 960px. Masked PII is a mono chip with an eye button, and unmasking asks for a reason.
  - **Analytics** (`admin.html#analytics`): one series per chart, in ink on a `#EFEDE8` track with 4px rounded data ends and a 2px gap between bars. Funnels are bar rows (label, bar, count, mono % of the first stage). Daily volume gets a hover tooltip and a "Data table" disclosure. Hidden counts (under the minimum group size) read `<10` in `--text-3`, never 0.
  - **Sentiment** (`admin.html#sentiment`): the Analytics chart rules, with one ink series of shares (the daily share of unhappy messages) and bar rows of label, bar, count, mono share. Hidden shares read `—`. The page is admin-only.
  - **Users and roles** (`admin.html#users`, `UsersAndRoles.tsx`): each row has plain dropdowns (sample customer, role), never its own Save. An edited row turns `#FFF8DC` (`tr.is-edited`, the same light as `tr.hl`) and says "Edited, not saved". A **table save bar** (`.save-bar`, `SaveBar.tsx`) appears at the right of the card's `.gc-h` once anything differs from what's saved: the count (plus how many are hidden by filters), one optional reason for the whole save, Discard (ghost) and Save changes (dark). Making someone an admin asks once more in the same button. Changes save one by one; a failed row stays edited with its `.err` line. The browser warns before leaving with unsaved edits. Reuse this bar for any table that edits several rows.
  - **Vote reason** (`chat.html`): after a thumbs down, a `.vote-why` row under the answer footer, “What was wrong?” followed by four `.vbtn` choices. The chosen one gets an ink border. It's optional and disappears when the vote changes.
- **Footer:** a hairline top border, 13px `--text-3`, a 2fr + 3×1fr column grid and a legal row.
- **Focus:** `outline: 2px solid var(--ink); outline-offset: 2px` (yellow in Nocturne).

## 9. Motion

- Easing: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` and `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`. UI transitions run 150–200ms. Press feedback is `scale(.97)` over 160ms.
- Hover effects are gated behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- Reveal on scroll: `opacity` plus `translateY(12px)`, 600ms, with a 60–90ms stagger.
- Vignettes play **once** when revealed: replies rise in parts, cursors arrive, the slider knob glides, checks pop, the chart wipes in. Keyframes only define `from` with `backwards` fill, so the resting CSS is always the final state.
- Animate only `transform`/`opacity` (and the `translate` property). No animated `filter: blur`, `clip-path` or large box-shadows. Pause off-screen animation (blooms, the hero stack) with IntersectionObserver.
- Live controls (slider bars) get **no transition**, so they never lag the user's hand.
- Product UI is stricter than the landing page. See Product UI rules.
- Everything decorative sits in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. Under reduced motion, reveals become plain fades.

## 10. Accessibility and output

- Decorative visuals get `aria-hidden="true"`. Include a skip link, semantic landmarks and an `aria-label` on each nav and section.
- Real text meets AA contrast. Focus states are visible.
- At 375px: no horizontal scroll, h1 is 30px, grids are one column. Vignettes are simplified (dimmed extras dropped), never removed. The full mobile rules are in §12.
- Output: one self-contained HTML file with plain CSS tokens on `:root` and `[data-mode]`, Google Fonts and pinned Lucide from unpkg.

## 11. Product UI rules (chat.html onward)

Agreed on 28 Oct 2026 (`plans/chat-ui-plan.md`), applying the SDS grammar to the app pages. The landing page keeps its marketing motion and pills. The palette doesn't change.

- **Named colours only.** Every colour is a `var(--…)` on `:root`. A value that's already in use gets a name; no new values are added.
- **Pills are for read-only labels:** `.rec-badge`, status `.pill`, verification. Anything pressable is a rectangle: chips and "Jump to latest" at `--radius-btn` (10px), source tags at `--radius-sm` (6px). Toggle switch tracks stay rounded.
- **Radii:** `--radius-xs` 4 (bars, tracks), `--radius-sm` 6, `--radius-md` 8 (rows, icon buttons), `--radius-btn` 10, `--radius-panel` 14 (answer cards, thinking panel), `--radius-lg` 16 (composer, starters, bubble), `--radius-pill`.
- **Type steps:** 11 (`--text-eyebrow`), 12 (`--text-micro`), 13 (`--text-xs`), 14 (`--text-sm`), 15 (`--text-body`). Short labels may use 11–12px, but any sentence is at least 13px. 16px is kept only for the mobile textarea.
- **Elevation:** hairlines and ground change. Answer cards, starters and the active chat have no shadow. `--shadow-float` is the only shadow, used for things that float: the composer, "Jump to latest" and the drawers.
- **Motion:** presses change colour or border, never scale. Messages and cards fade in without moving. Drawers slide, and the typing dots and caret stay as progress indicators. A global reduced-motion safety net is the floor.
- **Facts are ink:** amounts, rates, dates, counts and statuses use `--text-1`. Explanations use `--text-2`. `--text-3` (3.1:1 on `--bg`) is only for placeholders, kbd hints, icons and the thinking panel.
- **Status says its name.** Savings access shows a word ("Anytime", "At maturity") next to the bars, and computed figures are labelled "Estimate".
- **Never truncate a figure.** Grids holding figures use `minmax(0, 1fr)`. On narrow screens the comparison table restacks (each row label on its own line, the options under it) instead of scrolling. Names and titles truncate, with the full text in `title`.
- **One primary per screen:** only the newest answer keeps an ink handoff button; older ones show as ghost.
- **Live route cards before the catalog** (`web/components/agent/RouteCards.tsx`): the live chat reshapes the checked answer's route list into the same `.opts` cards with `.rec-badge` and `.rec-why`, 2×2 for four routes and one column on phones. Until reviewed prices are connected, each card shows "Exact cost · Not verified here" instead of a figure, and the recommendation comes from the checked answer rather than from code. This is an agreed, temporary exception to the deterministic recommendation rule above; switch back when the catalog and calculators land.
- **Memory with consent** (`web/components/agent/Memory.tsx`): when Raiffy proposes remembering something, a `.gcard` under the answer reads "Remember this for next time? · You decide" and shows the kind (uppercase 11px label), the customer's own words and the quoted message, with equal "Remember" (ink) and "Not now" (ghost) buttons, the One-time allow pattern. After a choice the row says its outcome in words ("Saved. You can delete it anytime in Context."). The Context panel lists "What Raiffy remembers": kind, text, "Told … · kept until …", a trash icon button per item and a ghost "Forget everything" that asks once more before deleting.
- **Layers:** `--z-float` 30 for floating widgets, `--z-drawer` 50 for open drawers (the scrim sits one below). Open drawers are `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal`, the rest of the page is `inert`, Escape closes them and focus returns to the button that opened them.
- **Card hierarchy** (answer cards in `web/components/agent/AnswerCards.tsx`, the list card in `AnswerText`): the title is 16px/600 with a 13px `--text-2` subtitle naming its sources and the newest date ("2 sources · 28 Oct"), and the icon sits on a 28px `--tint-rec` tile in `--amber-ink`. Each card leads with one figure: 28px/600 ink (`--fig-lead`, 24px on phones) on a `--tint-hl` band with its label above (the monthly payment, a card's first fact); route and comparison lead figures are 20px (`--fig-sub`). Values are 15px/500 ink over 13px labels. On a comparison, the deciding row (total cost, then DAE, then rate) comes first, and its lowest value gets `--tint-rec` when every option has one. The recommended option keeps its tint and gains `--halo-soft`, and its badge carries a 6px yellow dot. On a routes card, the customer's own route carries a grey "Your choice" `.pill` (`--surface-2`): beside the badge when it's the recommended route, on its own when it isn't. The recommendation never moves to follow it. Checklist numbers sit on `--tint-rec` tiles. Yellow is only light: no yellow text, fills or buttons, and the share-of-income row is never coloured.
- **Context panel toggle** (`#toggle-ctx` in `chat.html`): on wide screens the right panel slides closed (200ms, none under reduced motion) and the chat takes the space; it can be closed from its own header too, and ⌘\ / Ctrl \ toggles it at every width. The choice is remembered per browser; first visits start open.

## 12. Mobile first

Agreed on 29 Oct 2026. Every page, customer and staff, is designed at 375px first and checked at 375 and 390px.

- **Mobile-first CSS.** The base styles are the phone layout. Wider layouts are added with `min-width` queries at the breakpoints only: 480, 640, 760, 860, 960 and 1100px, plus 1440px for the review workspace's third column. No `max-width` layout queries and no off-scale breakpoints (1199, 859, 600, 520…). Hover stays behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Converting a page means rewriting its layout rules this way, not stacking overrides on top.
- **Touch targets are 44×44px on every page**, staff pages included. The hit area counts, not the drawn box: a 32px icon button or a 28px chip keeps its look and reaches 44px with padding or an `::after` with a negative inset, under `@media (pointer: coarse)`. Hit areas never overlap, so stacked targets (footer links, list rows, chips) sit at least 44px apart, centre to centre. Links inside a sentence are exempt. A range input gets a 44px tall box with its 4px track centred.
- **Text:** nothing under 11px at any width (badges, pills and vignette micro-type were raised from 10–10.5px on 29 Oct 2026), and any sentence is at least 13px (§11). Decorative 3D hero art (the isometric stack) is exempt. On touch screens text fields are 16px, so iOS doesn't zoom, and 44px tall.
- **The first screen does the job.** At 812px tall, the page's main action is visible without scrolling: the sign-in form, the swipe card and its buttons, the composer. A brand panel shrinks to a short strip on phones (logo and one line) instead of filling the first screen.
- **Three mobile patterns:**
  1. **Tables become stacked cards under 640px.** Each row is a hairline card: the first cell as the title (still the real link), the status or key figure at the right of the title line, the other cells as label-over-value pairs with labels from `data-label`. Mono figures and durations stay on one line (`white-space: nowrap`). Comparison tables already restack (§11). A data table that has to stay a table (the repayment schedule) scrolls inside `.tbl-wrap` with its first column sticky.
  2. **Chip rows scroll sideways.** Filter, topic and follow-up chips stay on one row on phones: `overflow-x: auto`, scroll snap, no visible scrollbar, a 24px fade on the edge that has more, and the active chip scrolled into view. Up to 4 chips may wrap to a second row.
  3. **Vignettes fit; they don't crop.** At phone widths a vignette is laid out for its card's width, with no fixed pixel widths inside. Only the one bleeding fragment runs past the edge, and only under its fade mask, so a word or figure is never cut by a hard edge. Dimmed extras drop first (§10).
- **Drawers and sheets:** side drawers take at most 86vw over a scrim and bottom sheets go full width; both use `100dvh` and safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), and keep the composer above the on-screen keyboard.
- **Keyboard hints hide on touch.** `kbd` hints and "press Tab" copy are hidden under `(pointer: coarse)`.
- **How it's built:** every prototype carries the same touch core between `/* §12-touch:start */` and `/* §12-touch:end */` at the end of its first `<style>`: a `::before` hit area on every control under `(pointer: coarse)`, 16px fields and 44px sliders. The page's own part follows inside the markers (drawn sizes and gaps so neighbouring hit areas don't overlap). Change the core in every page at once. Tables as cards need `data-label` on each cell: the prototypes' `labelCells()` script copies it from the column headers.
- **Check it with `pnpm audit:mobile`** (in `web/`). It loads every prototype view at 375 and 390px with touch and reduced motion. It fails on horizontal scroll, targets under 44px, text under 11px and fields under 16px, warns on sentences under 13px, and writes `web/.mobile-audit/report.md` with full-page screenshots. `--only chat,console` limits it to some views and `--app http://localhost:3000` adds the public app routes. Add every new view to its list. The script can't judge layout, so look at the screenshots too.
