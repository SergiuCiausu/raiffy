Recommends, you decide

A guide with rules it can’t break

Customers trust advice that shows its working. Raiffy is built so that every answer can be checked.

  • A recommendation, with its reasons

    Alternatives appear side by side, with the same amount, the same term and the same fields. One is marked as recommended, with the reasons and when another option fits better.

  • A source for every number

    Rates, fees and limits link to the customer’s offer or to the published fee schedule.

  • Says when it doesn’t know

    Tax, legal and complex lending questions go to a human advisor, along with the context.

  • Suitability first, product second

    An offer only appears after an affordability check, and Raiffy explains why it appeared.
