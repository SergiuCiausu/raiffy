Raiffy inside Smart Mobile

The app opens Raiffy with a reference to what you're looking at. Raiffy explains and compares, then opens the app's own screens, where you review and confirm.

Demo path
  1. Find the overdraft card among the From Raiffy cards on Home
  2. Raiffy compares and recommends
  3. Ask for a lower monthly payment
  4. Apply in Smart Mobile, pre-filled
  5. Read the SECCI and sign with the SMS code
  6. See the status back in Raiffy
