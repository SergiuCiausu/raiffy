# Bank Buddy: 150 Did-You-Know Finance Posts

Oct 28, 2026 · @Sergiu

150 short, purely educational finance facts for Bank Buddy's daily card feed and social channels, grouped into 10 topics. None of them mention or promote a Raiffeisen product; each teaches one idea a customer can use.

- **Tone:** one idea per post, plain language, no pressure, no product names.
- **Format:** each fact works as a card headline ("Did you know…") plus one supporting sentence; translate to Romanian with a native speaker before publishing.
- **Fact-check before publishing:** posts that cite a Romanian rule or a specific limit (marked with "RO" in the Tag column) must be checked by compliance against the current law, since rules change. Examples use round illustrative numbers, not live rates.

## Money basics and compound interest

| 7 | …that a 1% difference in yearly cost compounds too? Over 30 years it can eat a large share of an investment's growth. | General |

| 12 | …that opportunity cost is what you give up by choosing one option? Money spent today can't grow for tomorrow. | General |
