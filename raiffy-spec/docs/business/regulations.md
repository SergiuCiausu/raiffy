# Business: Regulations

Most consumer protections don't apply to business credit, but the AI, data, AML and payment rules still do. Where a PFA is involved, check both sides, because a PFA is a natural person. Status as of October 2026; confirm with Raiffeisen legal before production.

## What changes from the individuals track

| Rule | Business track | Practical solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OUG 50/2010 consumer credit (and CCD2 when transposed) | Doesn't apply: a "consumer" is a natural person acting outside their trade or profession, so PFA business loans and company loans are excluded ([art. 7](https://notari.pro/oug-50-2010-contractele-de-credit/art-7-obiect-domeniu-de-aplicare-si-definitii)) | Still show the full cost and all commissions as a Raiffy rule; never cite consumer rights (14-day withdrawal) for business loans |
| BNR Reg. 17/2012 | The DTI caps (40% lei, 20% FX) apply only to consumer and housing credit for individuals; the regulation's foreign-currency lending chapter does apply to business borrowers ([Reg. 17/2012](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_31655/Regulamentul-BNR-nr-17-2012-unele-conditii-de-creditare.html)) | Use debt-service coverage, not DTI; for FX business loans, show the exchange-rate risk scenario |
| PFA borrowing for personal purposes | Consumer rules do apply | Router asks personal or business, and the answer states which rules apply |
| Unfair commercial practices (Law 363/2007) | Protects consumers | B2B communication still falls under misleading and comparative advertising rules (Law 158/2008) |
| GDPR | Applies to PFA data and to people's data in company chats | Same masking, retention and DPIA as the individuals track |

## Rules that apply to both tracks

| Rule | Practical solution |
| --- | --- |
| AI Act Art. 50 disclosure and Art. 5 prohibitions | Same disclosure; no pressure tactics on owners in distress |
| AI Act high-risk (creditworthiness), from 2 December 2027 | Annex III covers credit scoring of natural persons, which includes PFAs; keep the agent out of credit decisions, as in the individuals track |
| AML Law 129/2019 | KYB and beneficial-ownership checks stay in the bank's flow; refusal and silent flag for evasion questions |
| Payment services (OUG 209/2019) | Payments only through Smart Business or Raiffeisen Online IMM, with their own approval rules; note that micro-enterprises keep some consumer-level payment protections under PSD2 |
| Comparative advertising (Law 158/2008) | Same honest comparison template |
| DORA and BNR outsourcing | Same vendor register for the shared core |

## Business-specific rules to track

| Rule | Status (Oct 2026) | Practical solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| e-Factura | B2B since 2024, B2C since 2025; 5 working days from 2026; PFAs with a CUI are obliged, suppliers identified only by CNP became optional from 1 June 2026 (Law 88/2026) ([ContApp](https://contapp.ro/blog/e-factura/)) | Explain obligations from the tax calendar; refer to the accountant or invoicing provider |
| SAF-T (D406) | Small taxpayers since 2025; PFAs and practices on single-entry bookkeeping exempt ([Manager.ro](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/legislatie/raportare-saf-t-pfa-urile-si-ii-urile-cu-contabilitate-in-partida-simpla-nu-vor-depune-d406-24036.html)) | Same; check for 2026 changes before use |
| Micro-enterprise and PFA tax regimes | Changed for 2026 (see `docs/business/calculations.md`) | Explain only, dated config, accountant referral |
| State aid (de minimis) | Guarantee programmes such as BID are granted as de minimis aid | Mention that state aid already received can limit eligibility; the programme operator decides |
| Funding programmes' own rules | Each call has its own eligibility and deadlines | Answer only from the programme record, show status and the official link |
